How each one is made

Jaguaruana has woven hammocks for more than a century. Ours come from three family workshops in town, and each hammock passes through the same hands from loom to lace.

9

Days on the loom

The body is woven on a vertical wooden loom, one row at a time, with a tight weave that does not stretch out of shape after the first summer.

16

Pairs of end cords

The end loops are braided by hand and tied into the body, so the load spreads across the full width instead of pulling on a few threads.

1

Questions we get

Can I use it outdoors?

Yes, under a porch or a covered deck. Cotton fades and weakens in constant sun and rain, so take it inside when you are not using it, the same way people do in Ceará.

Why lie diagonally?

Lying across the hammock at an angle flattens the fabric under you, so your back stays straight. The 160 cm width is what makes that possible.

Will it fit two people?

It holds up to 150 kg, which covers two adults sitting. For two people sleeping, we recommend two hammocks.

Do the colors run in the wash?

The yarn is dyed before weaving and rinsed twice at the workshop. Wash it cold and alone the first time.
